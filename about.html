About Tile Talk Tees

Tile Talk Tees makes original, funny t-shirts for American mahjong players.

American mahjong (or mah jongg, as the card says) is more than a game. It's the standing date, the same table and the same ladies every week, the groans over the Charleston and the joy of a Joker at just the right moment. We wanted shirts that speak that language, so we started making them.

What we make

Our first batch includes 15 designs: everyday table humor like “Hand Me a Joker and Nobody Gets Hurt,” a “Tile Day” shirt for every day of the week, and holiday designs like “Merry Craksmas.” Every design is our own original artwork.

How to buy

We're a brand-new shop. Our shirts will be sold through Merch by Amazon, which prints each shirt when it's ordered and handles shipping. Our listings aren't live yet; check back soon, or email us and we'll let you know when they are.
