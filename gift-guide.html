A quick mahjong glossary

Not a player yourself? Here's what the jokes on our shirts mean.

American mahjong (mah jongg)
A four-player tile game played from a yearly card of winning hands.
Charleston
The tile-passing ritual at the start of each American mahjong game, where players swap unwanted tiles.
Joker
A wild tile in American mahjong. Everyone wants one, which is why nobody should get between a player and her Joker.
Craks
Short for “characters,” one of the three suits. Hence “Merry Craksmas.”
Dots
Another suit, with circles on the tiles. Hence “Dots the Season.”
152 tiles
The number of tiles in an American mahjong set, Jokers included.
